MADE IN SWEDEN

Founded in 2020, Westrom has come a long way in 3 years. Westrom was created because other instrument makers are usually based in USA or Asia. Our company stands as the only instrument maker to have their workshops in Sweden.
Founded by Per Gustav Weström 2020, we've come quite far. All of our products are made in Sweden, and use the highest quaily materials available. The wood in most of our guitars are made of wallnut wood, and use premium hardware such as Seymour Duncan pickups
